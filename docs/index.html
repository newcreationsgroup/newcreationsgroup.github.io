The Group

One group.
One standard:
delivery.

New Creations Group is a registered South African holding company whose divisions operate as focused businesses under one balance sheet, one governance framework and one standard of delivery.

The structure is deliberate. Clients engage a single accountable partner; behind that partner sits capability that spans the physical economy — building infrastructure, moving goods, producing food — and the knowledge economy, where our consulting division modernises how organisations run.

We are built for institutional clients: corporations, multinationals and government. That shapes everything from our compliance posture to how we report on a project.
